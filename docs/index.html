      Plain-Language Variable Glossary

      Point Elevation (elevation_m)
      Terrain elevation above sea level extracted from the 30-meter Copernicus DEM.
      Local Slope (slope_deg)
      Local inclination angle. Slopes above 25°–30° approach critical mechanical shear thresholds in saturated soils.
      Zonal Max Slope (slope_max_1km/3km/5km)
      Steepest terrain in the 1–5 km radius, capturing adjacent escarpments and valley headwalls.
      Topographic Relief (elev_relief_1km/3km/5km)
      Elevation range (max - min) in the buffer; acts as a macro-proxy for gravitational potential energy.
      Antecedent Rainfall (rain_3d/7d/15d_mm)
      Cumulative precipitation over 3, 7, and 15 days from daily CHIRPS 0.05° satellite-gauge observations.
      Zonal Areal Mean Rain (rain_3km_mean_3d/7d/15d)
      Spatially averaged precipitation within 3 km, buffering against localized satellite grid cell displacement.

      Scientific Audit & Operational Limitations Disclosure

      In accordance with academic integrity guidelines, empirical limitations and weak folds from the Phase 3 results audit are explicitly documented:

      East Khasi Hills (Fold B3) Weak Discrimination:

      Models achieved near-random discrimination on East Khasi Hills (ROC-AUC 0.430–0.453). Meghalaya's flat-topped sandstone/karst plateau exhibits fundamentally different geomorphological failure modes than the steep crystalline Himalayas of Sikkim.

      Kodagu (Fold A3) Small-Sample Footprint:

      Kodagu contains only 9 documented events in the inventory. While Logistic Regression detected 6 of 9 (66.7% recall), Random Forest suffered complete threshold collapse (0.0% recall, 0 detected).

      Random Forest Threshold Miscalibration:

      Random Forest failed the 80% operational recall target on 100% of spatial folds (average recall 15.4%–34.0%). Tree ensemble probability outputs collapse under spatial covariate shift, making global threshold transfer unsafe.

      Cross-Region Transfer Failure (Domain Shift):

      Models trained in the Western Ghats failed when transferred to the Himalayas (40.9%–47.0% recall). Elevation shift (>40% of SHAP attribution in transfer) prevents blind transfer across mountain belts without local recalibration.

      Presence-Unlabeled Evaluation Scope:

      Reported precision and F1 scores are evaluated strictly against 5 km-buffered landscape background locations, NOT verified stable ground. They must not be cited as real-world occurrence accuracy.

      ⚠ ACADEMIC RESEARCH PROTOTYPE NOTICE: This decision-support tool is an academic engineering prototype. It does NOT have legal authority to issue evacuation orders or public hazard alerts. Model scores output an Index of Relative Landslide Susceptibility (RLSI) ranking terrain-rainfall conditions against regional landscape background; they are NOT calibrated real-world probabilities of slope collapse. All emergency measures are strictly governed by the District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA) and local civil administration.
