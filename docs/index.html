      ⚑ Alert Center & NDMA Response Protocols

      Institutional four-tier hazard categorization mapped to National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) Standard Operating Procedures.

      Minimal Risk (RLSI < 0.25)

      Normal background environmental state. Stable hillside regolith with routine moisture drainage.

      • Maintain standard routine monitoring of community rain gauges.
      • Ensure hillside roadside ditches and cross-culverts remain clear of debris.
      • Standard transport movement permitted along all mountain highways.

      Advisory (0.25 ≤ RLSI < τ*)

      Elevated antecedent moisture. Soil pore pressures beginning to increase in convergent hollows.

      • Alert Gram Panchayat disaster scouts in vulnerable escarpment hamlets.
      • Conduct precautionary inspections of active road-cutting toe slopes.
      • District Emergency Operations Centre (DEOC) initiates 6-hour rainfall polling.

      Watch (τ* ≤ RLSI < 0.75)

      Operational threshold breached. Subterranean shear stress approaching critical failure thresholds.

      • Pre-identify shelters and stage rescue vehicles at taluk headquarters.
      • Deploy traffic regulation squads; prohibit night travel on vulnerable ghat roads.
      • Mobilize Quick Response Teams (QRT) and place earthmovers on standby.

      Warning (RLSI ≥ 0.75)

      Severe hazard condition. Monsoonal deluge on saturated steep terrain creates imminent failure risk.

      • DDMA executes targeted precautionary evacuations from identified high-hazard zones.
      • Implement complete traffic closures on critical mountain highway sectors.
      • Stage National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) & SDRF battalions at regional forward bases.

      Statutory Legal Boundary Notice

      Under the Disaster Management Act of 2005, only the District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA) headed by the District Collector possesses legal authority to issue mandatory evacuation orders or declare disaster emergencies. LEWDSS is a scientific decision-support system designed to empower human incident commanders with objective data.

      Variable Glossary & Physical Interpretation

      rain_15d_mm: 15-day cumulative rainfall acting as proxy for deep subterranean soil saturation and pore water pressure.
      slope_deg: Direct gravitational shear stress angle on hillslope colluvium. Slopes >28° approach critical shear angles.
      elev_relief_3km: Macro-scale topographic relief capturing gravitational potential energy across adjacent valleys.
      rain_3d_mm: Short-term storm pulse trigger that destabilizes already saturated soil layers.
      OPERATIONAL NOTICE: This decision support system provides a regional Relative Landslide Susceptibility Index (RLSI) ranking terrain-rainfall conditions against verified regional backgrounds. It is an engineering decision aid and does not replace official meteorological advisories issued by the District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA).
