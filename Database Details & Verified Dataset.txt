Part 1: Regional Landslide Early Warning & Decision Support System Architecture

Course: CSE276 - Artificial Intelligence Foundations
Project ID: SIH26001 · LEWDSS
Authors: Shaik Umar Farukh (12515413), Saripella Jaswanth Varma (12513322) | Section: K3P25IL
Target Regions: Region A (Western Ghats: Wayanad, Idukki, Kodagu) & Region B (Eastern Himalayas / Northeast: East Sikkim, West Sikkim, East Khasi Hills)

The application implements a multi-tiered geospatial data and machine learning architecture:

1. Geospatial & Topographic Ingestion Engine:
- High-Resolution Digital Elevation Model: Copernicus Global DEM GLO-30 (30m spatial resolution).
  Derived Terrain Metrics: Point elevation (m), slope gradient (degrees), aspect, terrain roughness index (TRI),
  and multi-scale zonal statistics across 1 km, 3 km, and 5 km radial circular windows:
  - Zonal Elevation Relief: max(elev) - min(elev)
  - Zonal Slope Percentiles: 90th percentile slope, maximum slope, mean slope.
- Multi-Temporal Antecedent Precipitation: CHIRPS v2.0 (Climate Hazards Group InfraRed Precipitation with Station data)
  at 0.05° (~5.3 km) spatial resolution. Computes cumulative rainfall over 3-day (short-term triggering),
  7-day (soil wetting), and 15-day (deep pore-pressure destabilization) antecedent temporal windows.

2. State-Space Latent Soil Hydrology (Dynamic Linear Model - DLM):
- Discrete-time state-space Kalman filter tracking latent soil saturation S_t:
    State equation:       S_t = phi * S_{t-1} + beta * P_t + eta_t,   eta_t ~ N(0, W)
    Observation equation: Y_t = S_t + epsilon_t,                       epsilon_t ~ N(0, V)
  Parameters: Decay factor phi=0.88, precipitation impact beta=0.035, process variance W=0.002, observation variance V=0.015.
- Online filtering tracks real-time innovation, filter gain K_t, and unobservable antecedent moisture state.

3. Feature Engineering Framework:
- Feature Set 1 (FS1 - 5 point features): elevation, slope, rain_3d, rain_7d, rain_15d.
- Feature Set 2 (FS2 - 14 zonal features): FS1 + 1km/3km/5km elevation relief, 90th percentile slope, max slope.
- Feature Set 3 (FS3 - 17 spatial rainfall features): FS2 + 3km buffer mean rainfall (3d, 7d, 15d).

4. Machine Learning & Spatial Cross-Validation:
- Spatial Leave-One-District-Out Cross-Validation (LODO-CV): Rigorous hold-out protocol where models are trained
  on 5 districts and evaluated on the completely unseen 6th district to audit geographic generalization and domain shift.
- Logistic Regression (Balanced): Yields 87.0% generalized recall across held-out districts with stable, monotonic
  probability calibration (Brier score: 0.149; AUC-ROC: 0.778).
- Random Forest: 100 estimators (max_depth=6) demonstrating high local discriminative ranking (Wayanad local AUC: 0.887)
  with audited empirical threshold collapse across distinct geomorphological domains (Western Ghats to Himalayas).

5. Explainable AI (XAI) & GeoGuard RAG AI Assistant:
- SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations): TreeExplainer and KernelExplainer computing exact local additive feature
  attributions phi_i(x) for every evaluated observation.
- GeoGuard RAG Engine: Geotechnical Knowledge Base containing 15+ curated reference documents (IS 14458, GSI NLSM,
  USGS Landslide Guidelines, CHIRPS specifications) indexed with BM25 + dense TF-IDF keyword vector retrieval.


Part 2: Verified Dataset Sources & Direct Links

All datasets utilized in this project are strictly from official, peer-reviewed, open-access repositories:

1. Landslide Inventories & Geological Catalogs
NASA Global Landslide Catalog (GLC):
Dataset: https://data.nasa.gov/Earth-Science/Global-Landslide-Catalog/dd9e-wu2v
NASA Open Data: https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/nasa-global-landslide-catalog
Description: NASA Cooperative Open Online Landslide Repository (COOLR) covering rainfall-triggered landslide events (2004–2022).

Geological Survey of India (GSI) National Landslide Susceptibility Mapping (NLSM):
Link: https://bhukosh.gsi.gov.in/
Description: Macro-scale (1:50,000) landslide susceptibility mapping of India covering Western Ghats and Himalayan belts.

2. Digital Elevation Models & Remote Sensing
Copernicus Global DEM GLO-30 (30m Spatial Resolution):
AWS Open Data Registry: https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/
ESA Copernicus Data Space: https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/
Description: High-resolution global digital surface model derived from WorldDEM TanDEM-X mission.

USGS EarthExplorer:
Link: https://earthexplorer.usgs.gov/
Description: Open satellite imagery, SRTM 30m, and global elevation grids.

3. High-Resolution Meteorological & Rainfall Products
CHIRPS v2.0 (Climate Hazards Center, UC Santa Barbara):
Dataset HTTP Archive: https://data.chc.ucsb.edu/products/CHIRPS-2.0/
UC Santa Barbara CHC Portal: https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps
Description: Daily quasi-global precipitation product (0.05° resolution) combining satellite infrared with in-situ station observations.

India Meteorological Department (IMD) Pune National Data Centre:
Link: https://www.imdpune.gov.in/
Description: Gridded daily rainfall datasets (0.25° x 0.25°) and monsoon climate observation reports.

4. Disaster Management Support Portals
ISRO Bhuvan Disaster Management Support Services (DMSS):
Link: https://bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in/
Description: Near-real-time satellite-derived flood and landslide emergency monitoring by the National Remote Sensing Centre (NRSC).

National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), Government of India:
Link: https://ndma.gov.in/
Description: Official national guidelines, disaster mitigation protocols, and hazard zone maps.
